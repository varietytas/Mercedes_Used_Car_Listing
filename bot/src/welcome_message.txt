Hello there!
I am a bot for convenient navigation through the analysis of <b>Mercedes Used Car Listing</b> dataset.

<b>How to use me:</b>

-> You can choose what sections of the analysis you want to be shown by sending respective commands.
The list of commands and their description can be found in the bot <b>Menu</b>.

-> Sections <b>Structure</b> and <b>Overview</b> are also avaliable by buttons.

-> By clicking <b>Mean price over years</b> you can learn the average car price for the provided year range.

Enjoy!
